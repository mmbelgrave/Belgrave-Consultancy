Privacy

How I handle your data.

Short version: I collect only what I need to reply to you, send you what you asked for, and deliver what you buy. I do not sell your data, and this website sets no cookies.

2. What I collect, why, and on what basis

Email and enquiries

If you write to me by email, I see your name, email address and what you write. I use them to reply to you. Basis: your request, and my legitimate interest in answering business enquiries. I keep correspondence for as long as the conversation or any resulting work lasts. Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted after 12 months.

Free ERP resources (readiness scorecard and project costing checklist)

Your email address, so I can give you the PDF. If you tick the optional box and confirm your email address, I also send you follow-up emails, and you can unsubscribe at any time with the link in each email. If you do not tick the box, I do not add you to the follow-up list, and I keep your email address for 3 months and then delete it. Basis: your request for the tool, and, for follow-up emails, your consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier use. I keep a record of your consent, including the date and the wording shown to you, for 3 years after your most recent consent decision.

Bid Advisor interest form

I collect your email address, your answer and any comments you write about what you would expect. Formspree (formspree.io) passes your answers to my inbox, and MailerLite holds your email address. I use your comments to decide what to build, and your email address to tell you when there is something to try. Basis: your consent.

Buying the guide

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the seller of record. It collects your payment and billing details, issues the invoice and handles VAT. It sends me the details I need to support your order. Lemon Squeezy's own privacy notice applies to what it collects.

WhatsApp

If you message me on WhatsApp, I see your number and what you write. WhatsApp's own terms apply to the service.

3. Who else handles your data

  • Hosting: this website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It receives technical information, including your IP address, when you load a page.
  • Email delivery and lists: MailerLite, for the free tools and Bid Advisor emails.
  • Bid Advisor form delivery: Formspree (formspree.io), which passes your answers to my inbox.
  • Payments: Lemon Squeezy.
  • Email and records: the email provider for info@belgraveconsultancy.com, and my accountant, for the records the company must keep.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, they rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision. I make no decisions about you by automated means.

4. Cookies

This website sets no cookies and uses no advertising trackers. For simple visitor statistics (which pages are read, from which country, on which type of device) it uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service that sets no cookies, does not follow you across sites and does not store your full IP address or any personal profile. GoatCounter is operated from Ireland and keeps its data on servers in Finland and Germany. Fonts are served from this website, not from a third party. If that changes, I will update this notice and ask for your consent first where the law requires it. Lemon Squeezy, LinkedIn and other sites I link to are separate sites with their own notices and policies.

5. Your rights

You can ask me for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. Email info@belgraveconsultancy.com. You also have the right to complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (www.cnpd.pt), or to the authority in your own country.

6. Security and changes

I use reputable providers and keep access to your data limited. I may update this notice; the version on this page applies from the date shown above.
