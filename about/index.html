About

Mwata Belgrave

My career has taken me from project engineering to operations leadership. I now work independently with management teams on the decisions that connect business direction, people and delivery.

For more than fourteen years I worked at KCI Offshore Engineering, progressing through project and management roles. From 2015 to 2021 I was Director Operations on the executive management team, responsible for the technical, projects and project services departments. I also led two ERP implementations from selection to go-live.

I have worked independently since 2022 and am now based in Portugal, supporting clients across Europe in English and Dutch. My advisory work includes supporting the management team at KCI.

That operational background shapes how I work: understand the situation, make the choices clear and help the people responsible carry them through.
