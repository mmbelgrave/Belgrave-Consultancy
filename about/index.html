About

Mwata Belgrave

I started as a trainee engineer, became a project manager, and ended up running operations at an offshore engineering company. I advise from every level I have worked at, from the board to the project floor.

For more than fourteen years I worked inside KCI, an offshore engineering company: as project engineer and project manager, then Manager Projects, and from 2015 to 2021 as Director Operations on the executive management team, responsible for the technical, projects and project services departments. I also ran two complete ERP implementations, from selection to go-live.

Since 2022 I have worked independently as Belgrave Consultancy, from Portugal, advising management teams across Europe. I work with KCI as senior advisor to its management team.

I write the practical guides and tools published under Products from the same experience.
