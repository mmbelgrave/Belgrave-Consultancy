Free tools

Find out where you really stand, before you talk to a vendor.

Two practical tools I use myself. Each takes about fifteen minutes. Score yourself honestly, then compare scores across your leadership team: the differences are as useful as the totals.

Free assessment · PDF

ERP Implementation Readiness Scorecard

For anyone considering an ERP. Score seven dimensions from 1 to 5 and read your readiness tier out of 35.

  • Process clarity, objective clarity, leadership commitment
  • Internal capacity, data quality, budget realism, change readiness
  • Four readiness tiers, from “ready to start” to “stop and regroup”
  • Each dimension points to the chapter of the guide that covers it

I use your email only to send the scorecard and, if you tick the box, follow-up emails. Privacy

Free discovery tool · PDF

Project Costing Discovery Checklist

For engineering and project-driven businesses, before ERP workshops begin. Score seven dimensions of cost visibility out of 35.

  • Cost visibility level, change control, committed cost, labour time
  • Cost code structure, forecast to complete, ownership and action
  • Written for finance leads and project managers to complete separately, then compare
  • Shows what to settle before you configure anything
