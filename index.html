Where I stand

Control is lost in the gaps.

I work where strategy meets execution: close to management, with a clear view of the board above and the project teams below.

Many advisers stay at one level. I work across all three, because that is where most companies lose control: in the gaps between what is decided, what is communicated and what is actually delivered.

How I help

Four areas where I can take hold quickly.

Each starts with the business situation, not a framework. I deliver documents, templates and routines, not just advice.

  1. 01

    Project control and turnaround

    Restoring grip on project performance and financial results.

    • Estimate-at-completion control and forecasting discipline
    • Early warning: out-of-cycle alerts, peer reviews, weekly burn tracking
    • Management-of-change governance and authorisation matrices
    • Turnaround plans for board and management, with clear all-staff communication
  2. 02

    Commercial strategy and proposals

    Turning engineering capability into propositions clients want to buy.

    • Pricing and partnership structures: volume models, capacity reservation, engineering subscriptions
    • Compact, consistent proposal templates with a locked structure
    • Messaging that links upfront engineering investment to downstream savings
  3. 03

    Systems and ERP

    Selecting and implementing the tools the business runs on.

    • ERP selection and implementation, from the business side, to go-live
    • Gate-based vendor evaluation: weighted criteria, three-year total cost of ownership, go/no-go gates, test scripts
    • Document management selection and readiness
    • Practical guidance for teams doing this for the first time, including my ERP guide
  4. 04

    Governance and organisation

    Making the organisation resilient, not dependent on a few people.

    • Role and responsibility design, such as head-of-product-line structures
    • Management communication and decision frameworks
    • Future-state strategy and operating-model work

Interim and project-control assignments are available too: hybrid or remote, with time on site at key moments. Hours per week by agreement. Netherlands-based clients welcome; I work in English and Dutch.
