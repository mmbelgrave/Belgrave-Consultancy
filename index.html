Consultancy

Where I can help.

I work where strategy meets execution, close to management, with a clear view of the board above and the project teams below. Most companies lose control in the gaps between what is decided, what is communicated and what is delivered.

  1. 01

    Project performance and control

    When forecasts arrive late, margins move quietly and nobody owns the early warning.

    You getForecasting discipline, early warning and reporting that management and board can act on.

    What this includes
    • Estimate-at-completion control and forecasting discipline
    • Out-of-cycle alerts, peer reviews and weekly burn tracking
    • Management-of-change governance and authorisation matrices
    • Turnaround plans for board and management, with clear all-staff communication
  2. 02

    Commercial strategy and proposals

    When strong engineering is priced and proposed in ways that do not reflect its value.

    You getPricing and partnership models, and a consistent proposal structure your team can reuse.

    What this includes
    • Volume models, capacity reservation and engineering subscriptions
    • Compact proposal templates with a locked structure
    • Messaging that links upfront engineering investment to downstream savings in fabrication, installation and offshore time
  3. 03

    Systems and ERP

    When a system decision is coming and the business side is not ready for it.

    You getA selection and implementation run from the business side, from first requirements to go-live.

    What this includes
    • Gate-based vendor evaluation: weighted criteria, three-year total cost of ownership, go/no-go gates and test scripts
    • ERP and document-management selection and readiness
    • Hands-on support for teams doing this for the first time
  4. 04

    Governance and organisation

    When roles, authority and decisions depend on a few people.

    You getClear ownership and decision frameworks, so the organisation keeps working without its heroes.

    What this includes
    • Role and responsibility design, such as head-of-product-line structures
    • Management communication and decision frameworks
    • Future-state strategy and operating-model work

Selected experience

What the work looks like in practice.

Offshore wind and energy engineering · KCI Offshore Engineering, part of Sif Group

Senior advisor to the management team

Context

A specialist engineering company working through financial recovery, organisational change and the strategy for its next phase.

My role

Working directly with management, the group board and project teams, and coaching newly appointed managers.

The work
  • Project control: estimate-at-completion control, weekly burn tracking, early-warning alerts, peer reviews
  • Governance: management of change, authorisation matrix, product-line organisation
  • Commercial: proposal process, pricing and partnership models
  • Systems: ERP and document-management selection and implementation
What was delivered
  • Reporting to management and board
  • A redesigned change process and authorisation matrix
  • Standard proposal templates
  • A new business plan and departmental plans
