Where I can help

Does any of this sound familiar?

You may already know what needs to change. The difficulty is finding the time, making the choices and getting the organisation to work differently.

We've grown, but too much still depends on the same few people.

Decisions wait, experienced staff are constantly interrupted, and new managers are unsure what they can decide. I help clarify responsibilities and set up management routines, so the organisation can support its new size.

Organisation and management support

The workload has changed. Our cost base hasn't.

The structure was built for a different order book, owner or market. You need to adapt without losing skills the business will need again. I help compare workload, capacity and costs, work through the options and plan the changes with your management team.

Adapting the organisation

We have a new direction, but everyday decisions haven't changed.

Priorities compete, budgets still follow the old plan, and people are unsure what the new direction means for their work. I help turn strategic choices into a plan, with clear responsibilities, resources and follow-up.

Strategy into action

We're busy, but margins and cash are harder to explain.

Scope changes go unpriced, forecasts arrive late, and completed work can wait too long to be billed. I help strengthen project control and management reporting, so you can see where performance is changing and decide what to do about it.

Project and commercial performance

We need better systems, but can't afford to disrupt the business.

Teams rely on separate spreadsheets and disagree about what a new system should do. The people needed for implementation are already busy. I help prepare the business, agree requirements and lead the business side of selection and implementation.

Working together

Start with the decision or difficulty in front of you.

Tell me what has changed, where the business is getting stuck and what you need to decide. We can start with a conversation to understand the situation and whether my experience is relevant.

If there is a useful next step, I will propose a scope, the people involved and what the work should produce. We agree the approach and fees before starting.

Independent advice

A sounding board for managing directors and management teams: challenge assumptions, work through difficult choices and prepare for important discussions.

A defined assignment

Interim support
