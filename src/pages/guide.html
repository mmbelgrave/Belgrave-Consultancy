Is it for you?

Who this guide is for, and who it is not for.

This guide is for you if

  • You have decided, or are about to decide, to implement an ERP.
  • You run or lead operations in a growing business of roughly 30 to 250 people.
  • You want the preparation right before vendor demos begin.
  • You have been through one before, found it harder than it should have been, and want to do it differently.

It is probably not for you if

  • You are looking for a comparison of ERP software brands.
  • You need technical configuration instructions for a specific system.
  • You are deep into a struggling implementation and need help now. Talk to me instead.

The problem it solves

The hard part is not the software.

Most ERP guides are written by vendors, technology consultants or academics. They explain the software.

But the hard part of an ERP implementation is knowing your own business clearly enough to tell the software what to do, budgeting for the time it really takes, and bringing your people with you.

That work is almost entirely under your control. This guide shows you how to do it.

“The software cannot save a business that does not understand its own processes.”

Questions

Before you buy.

Is this guide about a specific ERP system?

No. It is deliberately vendor-neutral and applies to any ERP, or any business-critical system that touches several departments.

My business is smaller than 30 people, or larger than 250. Is it still useful?

The principles apply at any size. The examples and team structures are written for growing businesses of roughly 30 to 250 people.

We are already mid-implementation. Is it too late?

No, the principles apply at every stage. But if you need help with a live project now, a conversation with me will be more useful than the guide alone.

How long does it take to read?

The leadership boxes take under an hour. The full guide is designed to be read once and then used as a reference throughout the project.
