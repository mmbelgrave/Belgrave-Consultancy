About me   My consultancy

Is this guide about a specific ERP system?

No. The guide is vendor-neutral and focuses on the business preparation and decisions involved in ERP implementation. Its principles may also help with systems used across several departments.

My business is smaller than 30 people, or larger than 250. Is it still useful?

The examples and team structures are designed for businesses of roughly 30 to 250 people. Smaller or larger organisations may still find the principles useful, but will need to adapt them to their circumstances.

We are already mid-implementation. Is it too late?

No, the principles apply at every stage. But if you need help with a live project now, a conversation with me may be more useful than the guide alone.

How long does it take to read?
