Making the organisation fit the strategy, whether it has to grow into it or shrink to it.

You may recognise

  • Decisions wait for the same few people, and nobody is sure who decides what.

How I help

  • Organisation strategy for companies that have outgrown their structure, or carry too much of it
  • Roles, responsibilities, mandates and decision rights, including product-line ownership models
  • Capacity and resourcing review against budget and backlog
  • Leading change: sequencing, communication to each audience, follow-through
  • Preparing for the next phase: growth, restructuring, a change of ownership or a new operating model
  • Protecting the knowledge and the people you cannot afford to lose

You get

  • A target organisation with clear roles, mandates and decision rights
  • A capacity view set against budget and backlog
  • A sequenced change plan, with the messages for board, key team and all staff

06

AI and the future operating model

Seeing where AI can make your company more efficient, and organising for it.

You may recognise

  • Everyone says we should use AI. Nobody can say where it would pay.
  • We ran a pilot and it never reached daily work.
  • Our knowledge sits with a few experts, and I worry about confidentiality.
  • Competitors and clients ask what we are doing with AI.

How I help

  • An AI opportunity scan: where AI could save time or create value in your processes, ranked by business case
  • A readiness check: documented processes, data quality, ownership and skills
  • A practical roadmap: start with proven, business-grade tools, measure, then scale what works
  • Guardrails for confidentiality, personal data (GDPR) and responsible use
  • Roles and organisation for AI-enabled work, and bringing people with you

You get

  • A short list of use cases with a business case and an owner for each
  • The readiness gaps, and a plan to close them
  • Guardrails your people can follow, and a plan for a first pilot

I am independent of tool vendors: I help you decide where AI fits, not sell you a tool. The groundwork is the same as for an ERP: documented processes, clean data and clear ownership. I am also building Bid Advisor, an AI agent for bid and proposal work, so I know what it takes to put AI to work.
