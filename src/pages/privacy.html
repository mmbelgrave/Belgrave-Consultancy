2. What I collect, why, and on what basis

Email and enquiries

If you write to me by email, I see your name, email address and what you write. I use them to reply to you. Basis: your request, and my legitimate interest in answering business enquiries. I keep correspondence for up to 12 months after our last contact, unless you become a client. For clients I keep it for as long as the work lasts and as long as the accounting rules require.

Free ERP resources (readiness scorecard and project costing checklist)

Your email address, so I can give you the PDF. If you tick the optional box and confirm your email address, I also send you follow-up emails, and you can unsubscribe at any time with the link in each email. If you do not tick the box, I do not add you to the follow-up list, and I keep your email address for 3 months and then delete it. I keep your email address on the follow-up list until you unsubscribe. Basis: your request for the tool, and, for follow-up emails, your consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier use. I keep a record of your consent, including the date and the wording shown to you, for 3 years after your most recent consent decision.

Bid Advisor interest form

I collect your email address, your answer and any comments you write about what you would expect. Formspree (formspree.io) passes your answers to my inbox, and MailerLite holds your email address if you tick the box. I use your comments to decide what to build. If you tick the optional box, I also add your email address to the Bid Advisor list and tell you when there is something to try. If you do not tick it, I use your email address only to follow up on your answer. I keep your answer and comments for up to 12 months after our last contact, and your email address on the list until you unsubscribe. Basis: your consent for the list, and my legitimate interest in understanding what is useful for the rest.

Buying the guide

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the seller of record. It collects your payment and billing details, issues the invoice and handles VAT. It sends me the details I need to support your order. Lemon Squeezy's own privacy notice applies to what it collects. I keep order and invoice records for as long as tax law requires in Portugal, which is generally 10 years. Basis: the contract and my legal obligations for tax and accounting.

Withdrawal form

If you use the withdrawal form, I collect your name, email address and order number. Formspree passes them to my inbox. I use them only to handle your withdrawal and refund. The record of the withdrawal and of the order stays for as long as tax law requires in Portugal, generally 10 years, because I have to be able to show what was asked for and what was refunded. Basis: my legal obligations and the contract.

WhatsApp

If you message me on WhatsApp, I see your number and what you write. I keep the messages for up to 12 months after our last contact, unless you become a client. WhatsApp's own terms apply to the service.

4. Cookies

This website sets no cookies and uses no advertising trackers. For simple visitor statistics (which pages are read, from which country, on which type of device) it uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service that sets no cookies, does not follow you across sites and does not store your full IP address or any personal profile. GoatCounter is operated from Ireland and keeps its data on servers in Finland and Germany. Fonts are served from this website, not from a third party. If that changes, I will update this notice and ask for your consent first where the law requires it. Lemon Squeezy, LinkedIn and other sites I link to are separate sites with their own notices and policies.
